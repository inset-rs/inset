---
title: Layout
description: Rows, columns, lists and grids, laid out as Flutter lays them out.
---
Layout is Flutter's: constraints go down, sizes go up, and a parent places its children, as Flutter's [Understanding constraints](https://docs.flutter.dev/ui/layout/constraints) explains. So are the widgets:

- `Row`, `Column` (both with `spacing`), `Expanded`, `Flexible` and `Spacer`;
- `Padding`, `SizedBox`, `ConstrainedBox` and `AspectRatio`; `Center` and `Align`; `Stack` with `Positioned`; `Wrap`;
- `ListView` (`ListView::builder` for a long list), `GridView`, `SingleChildScrollView`, `PageView`, and `CustomScrollView` with slivers;
- `LayoutBuilder` and `MediaQuery` for the room there is;
- `Container`, `ColoredBox`, `DecoratedBox` and `ClipRRect`.

```rust
let notes = ListView::builder(|_app, _context, i| Some(Text::new(format!("Note {i}")).into_widget()))
    .item_count(100);
Column::new()
    .cross_axis_alignment(CrossAxisAlignment::Stretch)
    .children([
        Padding::new(EdgeInsetsGeometry::all(12.0)).child(Text::new("Notes")).into_widget(),
        Expanded::new(notes).into_widget(),
    ])
```
A grid of tiles at most 160 points wide, with room around it:

```rust
GridView::extent(160.0)
    .main_axis_spacing(8.0)
    .cross_axis_spacing(8.0)
    .children(tiles)
    .build()
    .padding(EdgeInsetsGeometry::all(16.0))
```
> `GridView::extent` and `GridView::count` take the tiles' settings; `.build()` answers the `GridView`, whose `padding` and scrolling settings follow it.
