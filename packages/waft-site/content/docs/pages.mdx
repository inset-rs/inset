---
title: Pages
description: Show a page over the others and go back, with Flutter's Navigator.
---
Pages are kept by Flutter's `Navigator`: `Navigator::push(app, context, route)` shows a page over the others, and `Navigator::pop(app, context, result)` goes back, `push`'s future answering the `result`, an `Option<Rc<dyn Any>>`. A phone's Back gesture pops the top page automatically.

- **WinUI:** `FluentApp` or the template's `WidgetsApp` holds the pages, each a `PageRouteBuilder`, which shows its page with no animation. A window's main pages are usually a `NavigationView`'s instead: the app shows the selected item's page as its content.
- **Cupertino:** `CupertinoApp` holds them, and a `CupertinoPageRoute` is a page that slides in from the right, its title what the next page's back button says.
- **Material:** `MaterialApp` holds them, and a `MaterialPageRoute` is a page that comes in as the platform's do.

```rust
// WinUI
let route = PageRouteBuilder::new(app, Rc::new(|_app, _context, _, _| Details.into_widget()));
Navigator::push(app, context, route.into_erased());
Navigator::pop(app, context, None);                          // in Details, back

// Cupertino
let page: WidgetBuilder = Rc::new(|_app, _context| CupertinoPageScaffold::new(Details).into_widget());
let route = CupertinoPageRoute::new(app, page).title(app, "Details".into());
Navigator::push(app, context, route.into_erased());
```
`Navigator::push` takes a `RouteRef`, the kept handle of any route, which `into_erased()` makes of the route's `Ref` ([Handles and ownership](/docs/handles)).

> `WidgetsApp` needs its `page_route_builder` (which `FluentApp`, `CupertinoApp`, and `MaterialApp` configure automatically): without it the app compiles, and panics on its first frame.

> Every app widget shows Flutter's DEBUG ribbon in its top right corner, over the controls there, until `debug_show_checked_mode_banner(false)` hides it.
